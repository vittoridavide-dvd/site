I also study political parties, decision-making preferences (direct democracy, technocracy, deliberative democracy), populism, and other political science topics that might qualify as nerdy.

Bio

At the moment, I'm based in Valencia, holding a postdoctoral grant from the Valencian Community for my project At the Roots of (Dis)Trust in Local Institutions. Before that, I spent five years as a postdoc and research fellow (chercheur à temps plein) at the Université Libre de Bruxelles, working on the ERC project Non-Elected Politics: Cure or Curse for the Crisis of Representative Democracy? (PI: Prof. Jean-Benoit Pilet). I also teach as an adjunct professor at various European universities.

I earned my PhD from LUISS University in 2019 with a dissertation on the rise of challenger parties in Southern Europe, focusing on Podemos, SYRIZA, and the Five Star Movement. That research became the foundation for my book, Southern European Challenger Parties Against the Mainstream: Podemos, SYRIZA, and MoVimento 5 Stelle in Comparative Perspective.

Oh, and outside academia, I try my best at padel.
